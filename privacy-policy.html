LANE RUSH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Lane Rush is a simple arcade driving game. This policy explains what information the game handles and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

Lane Rush does not require an account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play.

Local Game Data

The game may save your best score locally on your device using browser or WebView local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Ads and Analytics

Lane Rush does not show ads and does not use advertising ID. The game does not include third-party analytics SDKs.

Internet Access

The Android app may request internet access so the WebView version of the game can load required web game technology or content. We do not use this access to collect personal information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal information because Lane Rush does not collect personal information from players.

Children

Lane Rush is a general audience arcade game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the game changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.
